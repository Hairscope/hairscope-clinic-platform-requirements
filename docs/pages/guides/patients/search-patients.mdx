# Search for a Patient

> Quickly find a patient record in your clinic using name-based search.

## Prerequisites

- You have `patients.view` permission assigned to your staff role.

## Steps

1. Navigate to **Patients** from the main menu.
2. Locate the **search bar** at the top of the patient list.
3. Type the patient's name — you can search by:
   - **First name** (e.g., "Maria")
   - **Last name** (e.g., "Garcia")
   - **Full name** (e.g., "Maria Garcia")
4. Results update as you type, showing matching patients within your clinic.
5. Click on a patient to open their Patient Page.

## Notes

- Search results are scoped to your clinic only — you will not see patients from other clinics.
- The search matches against first name, last name, or the combination of both.
- If you cannot find a patient, verify the spelling or try searching by a different part of their name.
