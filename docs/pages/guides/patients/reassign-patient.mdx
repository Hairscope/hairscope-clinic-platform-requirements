# Reassign a Patient to Another Staff

> Transfer a patient's session assignment to a different staff member within your clinic.

## Prerequisites

- You are a **ClinicAdmin** or a staff member with reassignment permission.
- The target staff member is active and belongs to the same clinic.

## Steps

1. Navigate to the patient's **session** that needs reassignment.
2. Click **Reassign** (found in the session actions or assignment section).
3. Select the **new staff member** from the list:
   - Only active staff members in the same clinic are shown.
4. Confirm the reassignment.

## Notes

- Only the **assignedTo** field changes — the original attribution (who created the session, who captured images) remains unchanged.
- The reassignment does not affect the session's content, status, or any generated documents.
- The new staff member will see the session in their workload immediately after reassignment.
- Reassignment is recorded in the audit log.
