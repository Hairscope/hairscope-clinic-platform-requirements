# Capture Trichoscopy Images

> Capture high-magnification scalp images from mandatory and optional positions for detailed AI analysis of hair count, density, and thickness.

## Prerequisites

- You have an open session in **DRAFT** status.
- A trichoscope device is connected.

## Steps

1. Open the **DRAFT session** for the patient.
2. Go to the **Trichoscopy Images** section.
3. Select a **head diagram** — one of four views: `FRONT`, `LEFT`, `RIGHT`, `BACK`.
4. Place a **positional point** on the diagram to mark where you are capturing.
5. Capture the trichoscopy image.
6. Repeat for all mandatory positions (minimum 6 required):
   - **P1 — Frontal**
   - **P2 — Left Temporal**
   - **P3 — Right Temporal**
   - **P4 — Top of the Head**
   - **P5 — Crown**
   - **P6 — Occipital**
7. Capture additional images from other positions as needed.

## Important Notes

- **6 mandatory positions are required** before the session can be saved. All six (P1–P6) must have at least one image.
- **Additional images are allowed** beyond the 6 mandatory positions — capture as many as clinically relevant.
- Each image stores the **(x, y) coordinates** of the positional point you placed on the head diagram.
- The positional point is included in the generated report for reference.
- Once the session is saved, images are locked — no more can be added or replaced.
