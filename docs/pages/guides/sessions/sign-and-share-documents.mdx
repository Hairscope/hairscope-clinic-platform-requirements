# Sign and Share Documents

> Generate signed documents (Treatment Plan, Prescription) and share them with patients via multiple channels.

## Prerequisites

- The session is in **COMPLETED** status.
- You have a **digital signature** uploaded to your staff profile (required for Treatment Plan and Prescription).
- The session has recommendations (for Treatment Plan) or MEDICATION recommendations (for Prescription).

## Steps

1. Generate the document:
   - **Treatment Plan** — requires at least one recommendation of any type and your digital signature.
   - **Prescription** — requires at least one MEDICATION recommendation and your digital signature.
   - **Clinical Trichoscopy Report** — auto-generated on session completion, no signature needed.
2. Once the document is generated, share it via any of the following channels:
   - **PDF Download** — save a local copy.
   - **Email** — send directly to the patient's email address.
   - **WhatsApp** — share via WhatsApp to the patient's phone.
   - **Shareable Link** — generate a link accessible without platform authentication.

## Important Notes

- **Clinical Trichoscopy Report** can be shared immediately upon session completion — no signing required.
- **Treatment Plan and Prescription** can only be shared **after** they have been generated and signed. They are not shareable before signing.
- Shareable links remain valid for the duration of patient data retention.
- All sharing actions are recorded in the audit log.
- If you do not have a digital signature uploaded, you will be blocked from generating Treatment Plans and Prescriptions. Upload your signature in your staff profile settings.
