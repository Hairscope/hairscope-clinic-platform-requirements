# Add a Service to Your Catalog

> Create a new service offering in your clinic's catalog so it can be booked as an appointment and included in treatment plans.

## Prerequisites

- You have `catalog.create` permission assigned to your staff role.
- Your clinic's currency is already configured (Settings → Clinic → Currency).

## Steps

1. Navigate to **Catalog** from the main menu.
2. Click **Create New Item**.
3. Select the type **SERVICE**.
4. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Name** — the display name of the service.
   - **Description** — a brief explanation of what the service involves.
   - **Image** — upload a representative image for the service.
   - **Price** — the cost of the service (must be 0 or greater).
5. Set the **Duration** in minutes — this determines how long the service takes and is used by the scheduling engine to calculate available time slots.
6. Click **Save**.

## Notes

- **Qualified Staff** can be added after creation. Navigate back to the service item and configure which staff members are qualified to provide it (see [Configure Qualified Staff](./configure-qualified-staff.md)).
- **Currency** is inherited from your clinic settings — you do not set it per item.
- **Price** is required and must be ≥ 0. A price of 0 is valid (e.g., for complimentary consultations).
- The service will not be available for appointment booking until at least one qualified staff member is assigned.
