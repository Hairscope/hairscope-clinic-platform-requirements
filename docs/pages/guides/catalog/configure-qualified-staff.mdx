# Configure Qualified Staff for a Service

> Assign staff members who are qualified to provide a specific service. This is required before the service can be booked as an appointment.

## Prerequisites

- You have `catalog.edit` permission assigned to your staff role.
- The service item already exists in your catalog (type: SERVICE).
- The staff members you want to assign are active in your clinic.

## Steps

1. Navigate to **Catalog** from the main menu.
2. Select the **SERVICE** item you want to configure.
3. Go to the **Qualified Staff** section.
4. Click **Add Staff Member** and select the staff members who are qualified to provide this service.
5. Repeat for each staff member you want to assign.
6. Click **Save**.

## Notes

- **At least one qualified staff member is required** before the service can be booked as an appointment. Without qualified staff, the scheduling engine cannot assign the service.
- You can add or remove staff members from the qualified list at any time. Changes apply to all **future** appointment assignments — existing appointments are not affected.
- **Staff details are never exposed to patients.** The qualified staff list is used exclusively by the SmartScheduling engine to determine availability and assignment. Patients only see the service name and available time slots.
- To remove a staff member, open the Qualified Staff section and click the remove button next to their name.
