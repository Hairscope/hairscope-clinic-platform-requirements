# Add a Cosmetic or Supplement

> Create a cosmetic product or supplement entry in your clinic's catalog so it can be recommended in sessions and included in treatment plans.

## Prerequisites

- You have `catalog.create` permission assigned to your staff role.
- Your clinic's currency is already configured (Settings → Clinic → Currency).

## Steps

1. Navigate to **Catalog** from the main menu.
2. Click **Create New Item**.
3. Select the type **COSMETIC** or **SUPPLEMENT** depending on the product category.
4. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Name** — the product name.
   - **Description** — product details or usage context.
   - **Image** — upload a product image.
   - **Price** — the cost of the product (must be 0 or greater).
5. Add a **Routine** (optional but recommended):
   - **Frequency** — how often the product should be used (e.g., "once daily", "twice a week").
   - **Duration** — how long the patient should continue (e.g., "3 months", "ongoing").
   - **Instructions** — free-text application notes (e.g., "apply to scalp after washing", "take one capsule with breakfast").
6. Optionally add an **External Link** — a URL where the patient can purchase the product.
7. Click **Save**.

## Notes

- Unlike medications, the **routine is optional** for cosmetics and supplements — but it is recommended so that patients receive clear usage instructions in their treatment plans.
- When this item is recommended in a session, the routine defined here is copied as a starting point. The doctor can customize it per patient.
- Cosmetics and supplements appear in the **Treatment Plan** document (not the Prescription).
- Currency is inherited from your clinic settings.
