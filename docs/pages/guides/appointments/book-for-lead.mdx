# Book an Appointment for a Lead

> Schedule a consultation appointment for a prospective patient (lead) who has not yet been converted to a full patient record.

## Prerequisites

- Staff account with appointment create permission
- The lead must exist in the system and belong to your clinic

## Steps

1. Navigate to **Appointments** from the main navigation menu (or initiate booking from the lead's profile page).
2. Click **Book** (or **New Appointment**) to open the booking form.
3. Select **Lead** as the appointment recipient and choose the lead from the list.
4. Select the **Service** to be performed.
5. Choose a **date** — the calendar shows available dates based on clinic working hours and staff availability.
6. Choose an available **time slot** from the displayed options.
7. Click **Confirm** to finalize the booking.

## Notes

- The appointment is created with status **SCHEDULED** and stores the `leadId` on the record.
- An **email notification** is sent to the lead's email address confirming the booking.
- When the lead is later **converted to a patient**, the `patientId` is automatically updated on all active appointments (`SCHEDULED` or `CONFIRMED`) for that lead. The `leadId` remains for reference.
- The SmartScheduling engine assigns a qualified staff member automatically.
- Booking from the lead profile page pre-populates the lead's name and contact details.
