# Schedule a Next Visit

> Book a follow-up appointment for a patient immediately after completing their current visit.

## Prerequisites

- The current appointment must be transitioning to **COMPLETED** status
- Staff account with appointment create permission

## Steps

1. When marking an appointment as **COMPLETED**, the system offers an option to **schedule a next visit**.
2. Select the option to schedule a follow-up.
3. The booking form is pre-populated with the **same service** and **same patient**.
4. Choose a new **date** from available dates.
5. Choose an available **time slot**.
6. Click **Confirm** to create the follow-up appointment.

## Notes

- The next visit appointment is a **new independent appointment** — it is not linked to the completed one.
- All standard booking rules apply: slot availability, SmartScheduling assignment, email notification.
- You can **skip** next visit scheduling and simply complete the appointment without booking a follow-up.
- The SmartScheduling engine applies continuity of care rules, so the same staff member is preferred if available.
- Appointment completion is independent of session completion — completing an appointment does not automatically complete any linked session, and vice versa.
