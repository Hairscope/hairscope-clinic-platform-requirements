# Add Manual Line Items

> Add products or services to a draft invoice that are not part of the session's catalog recommendations.

## Prerequisites

- Staff account with billing edit permission
- The invoice must be in **DRAFT** status

## Steps

1. Open the **draft invoice** from the Billing section.
2. Click **Add Line Item** to open the line item form.
3. Enter the required fields:
   - **Name** — description of the item (e.g., "Pharmacy medication", "Additional consultation")
   - **Quantity** — number of units (must be greater than 0)
   - **Unit Price** — price per unit
4. The **total** for the line item is auto-calculated as `quantity × unit price`.
5. Click **Save** to add the line item to the invoice.

## Notes

- Use manual line items for products or services not in the clinic's catalog (e.g., medicines from a pharmacy, ad-hoc services).
- Manual line items can be **edited or removed** while the invoice is in DRAFT status.
- Manual line items are **never auto-removed** by the session recommendation sync — only auto-generated items are affected by sync.
- The invoice subtotal, tax, and total are automatically recalculated when line items are added, edited, or removed.
- Once the invoice is **FINALIZED**, no further edits are possible.
