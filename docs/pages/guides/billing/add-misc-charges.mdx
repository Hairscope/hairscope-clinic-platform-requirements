# Add Miscellaneous Charges

> Add one-off charges to a draft invoice that are not tied to a specific product or catalog item.

## Prerequisites

- Staff account with billing edit permission
- The invoice must be in **DRAFT** status

## Steps

1. Open the **draft invoice** from the Billing section.
2. Click **Add Charge** (or **Add Miscellaneous Charge**).
3. Enter the required fields:
   - **Description** — what the charge is for (e.g., "Late cancellation fee", "Express processing")
   - **Amount** — the charge amount
4. Click **Save** to add the charge to the invoice.

## Notes

- Miscellaneous charges are for one-off amounts not tied to a specific product or service in the catalog.
- Charges can be **edited or removed** while the invoice is in DRAFT status.
- The invoice subtotal, tax, and total are automatically recalculated when charges are added, edited, or removed.
- Once the invoice is **FINALIZED**, no further edits are possible.
- All changes are recorded in the AuditLog.
