# Manage Unassigned Leads

> Review and assign clinic ownership to leads that have no clinic assignment, ensuring no lead goes unattended.

## Prerequisites

- **OrganizationAdmin** role
- Leads exist with no clinic assignment (common in `MANUAL_ASSIGN` mode or when webhook payloads lack a `clinicId`)

## Steps

1. Navigate to **Leads → Unassigned** to view all leads without a clinic assignment.
2. Review each lead's details (name, source, contact info, selfie analysis report if available).
3. Check the **suggested clinic** — in `MANUAL_ASSIGN` mode, the suggested clinic (from the lead source) is pre-filled but editable.
4. Assign a **clinic** by confirming the suggestion or selecting a different clinic within your organization.
5. Click **Confirm** to complete the assignment.

Once a clinic is assigned, the **LeadDistributionAlgorithm** automatically assigns an active staff member within that clinic to the lead.

## Notes

- **Clinic-level staff cannot see** Unassigned Leads — this view is exclusive to OrganizationAdmins.
- In `AUTO_ASSIGN` mode, Unassigned Leads only occur when no clinic can be determined from the source (e.g., webhook with no `clinicId`, OrganizationAdmin manual entry with no clinic selected).
- In `MANUAL_ASSIGN` mode, all incoming leads from API sources start as Unassigned until the OrganizationAdmin confirms.
- You cannot assign a clinic from a different organization.
- The assignment is recorded in the AuditLog.
