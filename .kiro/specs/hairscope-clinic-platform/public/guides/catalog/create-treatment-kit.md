# Create a Treatment Kit

> Bundle multiple catalog items (services, medications, cosmetics, supplements) into a single treatment kit that can be recommended as a complete package.

## Prerequisites

- You have `catalog.create` permission assigned to your staff role.
- The catalog items you want to include in the kit already exist in your catalog.

## Steps

1. Navigate to **Catalog** → **Treatment Kits** from the main menu.
2. Click **Create New Kit**.
3. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Name** — the kit name (e.g., "Hair Restoration Starter Pack").
   - **Description** — what the kit includes and who it's for.
   - **Image** — upload a representative image.
   - **Price** — the kit price (must be 0 or greater).
4. Add catalog items to the kit:
   - Search for items by name.
   - Add any mix of types — services, medications, cosmetics, and supplements can all be combined in one kit.
5. Optionally **override routines** per item — customize the frequency, duration, or instructions for how an item should be used within this specific kit context.
6. Optionally add an **External Link** — a URL for purchasing or booking the kit.
7. Click **Save**.

## Notes

- The **kit price can differ** from the sum of individual item prices. Use this for bundle discounts or premium packaging.
- A kit **must have at least 1 item** — you cannot save an empty kit.
- When a kit is recommended in a session, all items and their routines are included in the Treatment Plan PDF.
- If the kit contains **MEDICATION** items, those medications will also appear in the Prescription PDF (requiring a digital signature).
- Currency is inherited from your clinic settings.
- Items can be added or removed from a kit at any time after creation.
