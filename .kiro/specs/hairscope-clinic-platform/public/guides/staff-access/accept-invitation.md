# Accept an Invitation

> Complete your registration after receiving an invite from your clinic admin.

## Prerequisites
- An invitation email from Hairscope

## Steps

1. Open the invitation email.
2. Click the invitation link.
3. If the link is valid (within 7 days), you'll see a password setup form.
4. Set your password:
   - Minimum 8 characters
   - Must contain at least one letter (a-z or A-Z)
   - Must contain at least one number (0-9)
   - Must contain at least one symbol (e.g., !@#$%^&*)
5. Submit the form.
6. You are now logged in with your assigned roles.
7. Complete your profile: first name, last name, phone, specialization, experience.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| "Invite Expired" | Link is older than 7 days | Ask your admin to resend the invitation |
| "Invite Already Used" | Password was already set | Log in with your email and password |
| "Invite Revoked" | Admin resent a new invitation | Use the newest invitation link |
| "Invite Not Found" | Invalid link | Contact your admin |
