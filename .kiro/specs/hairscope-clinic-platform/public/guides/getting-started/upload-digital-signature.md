# Upload Your Digital Signature

> Upload a signature image to your profile so you can sign treatment plans and prescriptions.

## Prerequisites
- Any staff account (active)

## Steps

1. Navigate to your Profile settings.
2. Find the "Digital Signature" section.
3. Click "Upload Signature."
4. Select an image file:
   - Accepted formats: PNG, JPEG
   - Maximum size: 2MB
5. The signature is saved to your profile.

## How It's Used

- Your signature image is attached to Treatment Plans and Prescriptions that you generate.
- The document displays your name, specialization, and signature.
- You cannot generate Treatment Plans or Prescriptions without a signature uploaded.

## Managing Your Signature

- Uploading a new signature replaces the previous one.
- You can delete your signature at any time (but you won't be able to generate documents until you upload a new one).
- All signature uploads and deletions are recorded in the audit log.
