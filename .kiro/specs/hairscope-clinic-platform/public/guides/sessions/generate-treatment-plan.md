# Generate Treatment Plan

> Create a signed Treatment Plan PDF containing all session recommendations with routines for the patient.

## Prerequisites

- The session is in **COMPLETED** status.
- The session has at least one recommendation (any type).
- You have a **digital signature** uploaded to your staff profile.
- You have permission to generate treatment plans.

## Steps

1. Open the **COMPLETED session** for the patient.
2. Go to the **Recommendations** section.
3. Click **Generate Treatment Plan**.
4. Review all recommendations with their routines on the review screen.
5. **Approve** the recommendations and confirm generation.
6. The system generates the Treatment Plan PDF, which includes:
   - Patient details.
   - Your name, specialization, and details.
   - Clinic details.
   - Session date.
   - All recommendations with routines.
   - Your digital signature image.
7. Share the Treatment Plan via:
   - PDF download.
   - Email to patient.
   - WhatsApp to patient.
   - Shareable link.

## Important Notes

- A **digital signature is required**. If you have not uploaded one to your profile, generation will be blocked.
- The Treatment Plan includes **all recommendation types** (services, medications, cosmetics, supplements, kits).
- The generating staff member's name and signature appear on the document — you are liable for the recommendations.
- If recommendations are edited after signing, the previous items are shown with strike-through formatting and the document must be re-signed (see [Edit After Signing](./edit-after-signing.md)).
