# Generate Prescription

> Create a signed Prescription PDF containing only medication recommendations with routines for the patient.

## Prerequisites

- The session is in **COMPLETED** status.
- The session has at least one **MEDICATION** recommendation.
- You have a **digital signature** uploaded to your staff profile.
- You have permission to generate prescriptions.

## Steps

1. Open the **COMPLETED session** for the patient.
2. Click **Generate Prescription**.
3. Review the medication items with their routines on the review screen.
4. **Approve** the prescription and confirm generation.
5. The system generates the Prescription PDF, which includes:
   - Patient details.
   - Your name, specialization, and **license number**.
   - Clinic details.
   - Date.
   - All MEDICATION items with routines.
   - Your digital signature image.
6. Share the Prescription via:
   - PDF download.
   - Email to patient.
   - WhatsApp to patient.
   - Shareable link.

## Important Notes

- **Only MEDICATION items** appear in the Prescription. Cosmetics, supplements, and services are excluded — those appear only in the Treatment Plan.
- A **digital signature is required**. If you have not uploaded one to your profile, generation will be blocked.
- Your **license number** is included on the Prescription (pulled from your staff profile).
- If the session has no MEDICATION recommendations, the Generate Prescription option is not available.
- If medications are edited after signing, the document must be re-signed (see [Edit After Signing](./edit-after-signing.md)).
