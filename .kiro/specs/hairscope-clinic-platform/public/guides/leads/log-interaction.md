# Log a Lead Interaction

> Record a communication or meeting with a lead to maintain a complete interaction history for the team.

## Prerequisites

- Staff account with lead edit permission
- Access to the lead's profile

## Steps

1. Navigate to the **lead profile** page.
2. Click **Add Action** to open the interaction form.
3. Select the **action type** from the dropdown:
   - WhatsApp
   - Email
   - Facebook Message
   - Phone Call
   - In-Person Meeting
   - Other
4. Enter the **content text** describing the interaction (e.g., summary of the conversation).
5. Optionally, change the **lead status** (e.g., from `NEW` to `CONTACTED`) as part of this action.
6. Click **Save** to log the interaction.

## Notes

- All actions are displayed in **reverse chronological order** (newest first) on the lead profile.
- Each action records: action type, content, timestamp (UTC), and the staff member who added it.
- Lead actions are preserved permanently — they are never deleted, even after lead conversion to patient.
- When a lead is converted to a patient, all lead actions remain accessible via the patient's history (referenced, not copied).
